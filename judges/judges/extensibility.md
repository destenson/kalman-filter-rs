**Ease of change.** Lead with the probe evidence: for each hidden follow-up feature,
read the diff and the maintainer notes. Did the change land in one obvious place, or
was it threaded through many files? Did the maintainer work with the design or around
it? Distinguish *seams placed where change actually happened* (good) from *speculative
generality that no probe used* (cost without benefit). Also judge test support: can
a change be verified cheaply?
