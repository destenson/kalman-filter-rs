New `src/observer.rs` with public `InnovationReport<'a, T>` exposing `innovation()` (y = z − Hx, prior x), `covariance()` (S, exactly symmetric) and `nis()` (yᵀS⁻¹y). `src/filter.rs`: the filter gains an optional boxed `FnMut(&InnovationReport<'_, T>)` plus one m×m S buffer, registered by `set_observer`, removed by `clear_observer`, with `has_observer`. In `update`, S is copied into that buffer only when an observer is registered (before `cholesky_in_place` overwrites `ws.s`); after `commit` succeeds, NIS is computed by solving with the factor still in `ws.s`, reusing the free `ws.rkt`, then the callback runs. No-observer path: two predictable Option branches, no copy, no solve, no allocation; numerics bit-identical. `src/lib.rs` adds `pub mod observer;` and the `linear` re-export; DESIGN_NOTES.md records the change, the migration note (the filter is no longer `Clone`) and the `'static` capture requirement.
New `tests/linear_kf_observer.rs` (6): one-step y/S/NIS against textbook values and an independent 2×2 solve; f32; bit-identical trajectories with/without observer; zero allocations per update on both paths; rejected updates report nothing and `clear_observer` stops reporting; mean NIS over 5,000 steps of a correctly specified m=2 model = 1.99965, inside 10%.
Easy: `commit` already marked the acceptance point and S's factor was still in `ws.s`, so NIS is one triangular solve reusing a free buffer.
Hard: `cholesky_in_place` destroys S, so an m×m buffer owned by the observer slot is filled just before factorisation, and only when an observer is registered; and the observer is a boxed FnMut, so the filter lost `Clone` (no honest clone of a stateful callback) and the `Copy` builder cannot carry one.
Verified: frozen 32, lib 114, full suite and doctests pass, zero allocations per update on both paths, mean NIS 1.99965.
Not verified: no wall-clock benchmark, so "costs nothing measurable" rests on zero allocations, bit-identical results and one branch, not timing.
